# Applicants under 18: what is built, and recommended follow-ups

## What is built

- **Who is a minor.** Decided from the date of birth: under 18. With no date of birth, a pupil at school
  (Primary, O-Level, A-Level) is treated as a minor until they say otherwise. Education level never makes
  someone an adult: a 16-year-old undergraduate is a minor.
- **When it applies.** A minor can browse, see recommendations and build a profile freely. Submitting an
  application (wizard or quick apply) is refused until the guardian's name, phone and relationship are given
  and the "parent or guardian is aware of and involved" box is ticked. The message lists what is missing.
- **What providers see.** On an application from a minor, the guardian's name, phone and relationship, taken
  at submission and kept with the application along with the recorded results and profile fields.

## Recommended follow-ups (not built)

1. **Confirm the guardian by SMS or email.** Today the guardian's details are what the applicant typed, and the
   tick is the applicant's own word. Send the guardian a one-time code or link that must be used before the
   application counts as confirmed. Needs: an SMS provider (or email), a consent record (who, when, which
   application), resend and expiry rules, and a wording the guardian can understand without an account.
2. **Retention rules.** Decide how long a minor's profile, guardian details and application snapshots are kept
   after a decision or after the account is closed, and delete or anonymise on that schedule. Include audit-log
   references and stored documents. Needs a policy from whoever is accountable for the data, not just code.
3. **Becoming an adult.** When someone turns 18 the guardian details stop being shown to providers on new
   applications (already true) but stay on past ones. Decide whether to notify the guardian-linked contact, and
   whether old snapshots keep the guardian.
4. **Guardian change after applying.** The snapshot keeps the guardian as given at submission. Decide whether
   a provider should be told if the applicant later corrects a wrong phone number.
5. **Date of birth at registration.** Registration asks no age, so a minor who never fills in their profile is
   only recognised by level. Asking for date of birth at registration would make the minor rule reliable.
6. **Provider-side safeguards.** Guidance for providers on contacting minors (through the guardian, not
   directly), and a flag in the provider's inbox for applications from minors.
